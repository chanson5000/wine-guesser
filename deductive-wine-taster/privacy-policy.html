Privacy Policy

We collect information voluntarily provided by the user, such as:

  • Email address
  • Username or Display Name
  • Wine tasting results

We may also collect performance and analytics data related to the application for development purposes.

We do not share data with third parties.

Deleting your data

  • To delete individual tastings, or clear your whole tasting history, use Conclusion History in the app.
  • To delete your account, open Profile and choose Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your saved tasting history. It cannot be undone.
  • If you no longer have the app, delete your account on the web by signing in with the same email and password.
